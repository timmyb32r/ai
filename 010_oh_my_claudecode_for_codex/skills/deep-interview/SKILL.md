# Deep Interview

1. Identify ambiguity across goal, users, scope, constraints, interfaces, data, failure behavior, operations, and acceptance criteria.
2. Ask one high-information question at a time; do not ask facts discoverable from the workspace.
3. After each answer, summarize the decision and update remaining uncertainty.
4. Introduce a contrarian question when assumptions appear too convenient and a simplification question when scope expands.
5. Stop when remaining ambiguity cannot materially change the result.
6. Produce a spec containing goal, non-goals, constraints, decisions, acceptance criteria, risks, and unresolved questions.
7. Wait for explicit approval before turning an interview-only request into implementation.
