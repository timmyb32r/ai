# Scientific Research

1. Define the research question, hypotheses, measurable outcomes, datasets, confounders, and stop conditions.
2. Establish provenance and an unchanged baseline.
3. Decompose independent analyses and run them in parallel where safe.
4. Use appropriate statistics, uncertainty intervals, effect sizes, and multiple-comparison controls.
5. Attempt falsification and cross-validation; distinguish exploratory from confirmatory results.
6. Preserve commands, parameters, seeds, raw outputs, and environment details needed for reproduction.
7. Report methods, findings, negative evidence, limitations, and the next most informative experiment.
